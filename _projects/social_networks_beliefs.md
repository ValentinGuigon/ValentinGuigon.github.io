description_paragraphs:
  - Building on my ReceivingNews and SendingNews projects, I developed a framework for studying belief updating in self-formed social networks with Jean-Claude Dreher, Frédéric Moisan, and Alain Barrat. Now led by Gaël Carniel, the study examines how displayed judgment certainty shapes whom people choose to observe and how they subsequently revise their beliefs about news.
  - Across eight online sessions, 273 participants evaluated 14 true and false news headlines, spent endowments to select information sources, and revised their beliefs over five updates. We show how collective belief formation depends on how people select their sources as well as how they integrate social information.
